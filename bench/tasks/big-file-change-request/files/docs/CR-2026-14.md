# CR-2026-14: roaming prices from 1 November

Approved by pricing on 2026-10-02. All changes are in `rating.py`.

1. **Norway, mobile data:** 0.10 per MB instead of 0.12. Norway's other prices
   (calls, incoming calls, texts, picture messages, video calls) don't change.
2. **Business plans:** the business "Gold" plan is renamed "Premium" (its name
   and its label; multiplier and included minutes stay). The business upgrade
   path follows the rename. The *personal* Gold plan stays exactly as it is.
3. **New country, Iceland** (code `is`, zone EEA): add it to `COUNTRIES` in its
   alphabetical place, with all six services priced exactly like Norway
   (including change 1), and register them in `RULES` like the other countries.
4. **Bug:** calls are billed per *started* minute (61 seconds = 2 minutes), but
   `_bill_minutes` rounds to the nearest minute. Fix it. The other billing
   helpers are correct and must not change.
5. **Fair use, far destinations:** the ROW2 fair-use volume goes from 500 MB to
   1000 MB. ROW1 stays at 500 MB.
6. `TARIFF_VERSION` becomes `"2026.4"`.
7. **Clean-up:** the last legacy contracts were migrated in 2025. Remove
   `legacy_flat_rate` and the "legacy" plan shortcut in `rate_event`; a plan
   named "legacy" is now an unknown plan (ValueError, like any other).

# The Tessera name and logo

Tessera's code and artwork are open source under the
[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): you may use, change and share them freely
within that licence. The licence doesn't give permission to use the **name
"Tessera"** for this project or its **logo** (the mosaic "T" and the app
icons in `apps/tessera/assets` and `apps/tessera/src-tauri/icons`) as a
name or mark for your own version; its section 6 leaves that out on purpose.
This page says how you may use them. The aim is simple: people should always
be able to tell the official Tessera from someone else's version.

## You may, without asking

- **Talk about Tessera**: in articles, reviews, videos, tutorials, talks and
  posts, including screenshots of it.
- **Say what works with it**: "works with Tessera", "a Tessera skill", "for
  Tessera", for your own software, bots or skills, as long as you don't
  suggest we made or approved it.
- **Share unchanged copies** under the Tessera name, for example as a package
  in a Linux distribution, with only the changes needed to package it, if you
  say who packaged it.
- **Use the logo to point to the project**, unchanged, for example next to a
  link to it.
- **Say your project is based on Tessera** ("a fork of Tessera", "built on
  Tessera").

## Please don't

- Call a changed copy of Tessera (a fork), or anything else, "Tessera", or
  give it a name, logo or icon that looks or sounds confusingly similar. Give
  your version its own name and icon, also in app stores and installers.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests we made, approved, sponsor or
  support your product, service or company.
- Offer a service under the Tessera name, such as a hosted "Tessera cloud".
- Use the name or logo in your company, product or domain name.
- Change the logo, or use it as part of another logo.
- Sell merchandise with the name or logo.

If you want to do something on this list, or aren't sure, ask in an issue
at https://github.com/KC-ctrl095/Tessera/issues. We say yes where we can.

## Other names

Other names that appear in Tessera and its documentation (Anthropic, Claude,
OpenAI, ChatGPT, Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft, Tailscale and others) are
trademarks of their owners. Tessera uses them only to say what it works
with, and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by them.
